Habito is a habit-building companion designed to help people turn intentions into lasting routines. Many people want to become more productive, healthier, and more disciplined — but the biggest challenge isn't knowing what to do, it's staying consistent. Habito solves this through personalization, structured daily missions, and gamification that keeps every day engaging.

## Contributions

- Built cross-platform mobile app with Flutter
- Developed backend REST APIs with Go
- Designed PostgreSQL schema for habits, streaks, and gamification
- Implemented gamification system (XP, levels, achievements)
- Built weekly reflection and social accountability features

## Impact

In development — aims to keep users consistent through structured daily missions, gamified progress, and shared accountability with friends.
